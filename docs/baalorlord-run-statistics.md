# Baalorlord run-state statistics

This report summarizes the reconstructed Standard Ascension 20 combat states in the local Baalorlord table. It describes decks at recorded fights; it does not estimate the frequency of a deck across all runs, establish why a card was chosen, or measure a policy. The `fight win` column is the share of these individual fight states marked won, not a run win rate.

## Coverage and method

The table contains **49,895 kept states** across four characters and 56 character × act × room-kind strata. It was reconstructed against Spire Gym revision `e3c13836bd1458be8a87b93160f5c350f78fcd80`. The table SHA-256 is `30e6e64441e3c5ec72d6c227982f583c47ed4535dcfdfe48a745cb99a2c4b44f`. Card types come from `gym_card_types` in `configs/neural/standard/name-aliases.json` (SHA-256 `83f22a0fdbb8241695aaf5c3ef9536f45c51846262221c7835415e15c804705c`), not from run-state rows.

Each state is one reconstructed deck at one fight. Deck size is summarized by the median and IQR (75th percentile minus 25th percentile), using linearly interpolated quantiles. Upgrade fraction and card-type mix pool card copies across states. A card or relic share counts the fraction of decks containing it at least once; duplicate card copies do not increase a card’s deck share. The lists show the 15 highest shares in each stratum (or fewer when fewer items occur), sorted by share and then ID. Shares are rounded to one decimal point in these tables.

Mean potions is the average number of recorded potions carried into the fight. **States tagged `potions_assumed_empty` are included**, with zero potions as recorded; the adjacent value reports their share. HP fraction is the mean of each state’s `hp / max_hp`. Card type labels are Attack, Skill, Power, Status, and Curse.

The adjacent reconstruction report gives these character drop rates, calculated as dropped / (kept + dropped), excluding scope exclusions: Ironclad **15.2%** (2,617 / 17,262), Silent **15.1%** (2,281 / 15,105), Defect **14.0%** (2,218 / 15,812), and Watcher **24.5%** (2,866 / 11,698). These drops can affect the observed composition, especially for Watcher; the aggregates describe retained states and should not be read as an unbiased census of every fight. Source: `artifacts/development/neural-combat/run-states/run-states-report.json`.

## What his decks look like

These broad readings pool all retained states for each character. Frequencies below are deck shares across that character’s states; the detailed tables that follow break the same kinds of statistics down by act and room.

### Ironclad

The pooled deck-copy mix is 44.7% skills, 40.0% attacks, 10.1% powers, and 5.1% curses. Bash appears in 94.1% of retained decks, Defend in 92.9%, and Strike in 84.0%; common additions include Shrug It Off (47.0%), Pommel Strike (41.0%), and Headbutt (39.7%).

### Silent

Skills make up 54.3% of pooled card copies, compared with 33.3% attacks, 7.8% powers, and 4.6% curses. Survivor and Neutralize remain in nearly every retained deck (99.8% and 99.1%); Backflip (46.4%), Piercing Wail (39.9%), Acrobatics (36.7%), and Blade Dance (35.7%) are frequent additions.

### Defect

The pooled card-copy mix is 52.5% skills, 31.1% attacks, 11.6% powers, and 4.8% curses. Dualcast, Zap, and Defend appear in 88.5–91.8% of retained decks; Hologram appears in 50.5%, followed by Coolheaded (42.0%) and Cold Snap (38.3%).

### Watcher

The pooled deck-copy mix is 45.5% attacks, 41.8% skills, 7.1% powers, and 5.6% curses. Vigilance (99.6%) and Eruption (96.2%) are nearly universal, while Cut Through Fate appears in 53.1%; Third Eye and Meditate appear in 34.9% and 30.7%. Watcher also has the highest reported reconstruction drop rate, so its retained-state summary needs the most caution.
