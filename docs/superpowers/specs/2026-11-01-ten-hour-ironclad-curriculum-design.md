## Intent and approaches

Expand playing experience beyond three decks while keeping an interpretable comparison. Alternatives were equal long allocations to all three arms, one mixed-only run, and a short matched comparison followed by long mixed continuation. The builder chooses the third: it spends most training time on the requested broad learner, while retaining fixed/randomized controls at an equal initial action/optimizer prefix. This allocation is a builder decision under the user’s ten-hour budget, not an empirically established optimum.
